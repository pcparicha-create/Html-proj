Learn Some Things About Chess

Chess is a battle where the soldiers never complain, even when you boldly sacrifice them for “strategy.”

Every piece has its own personality: knights jump around like hyper kids, while rooks act like they’re late for an appointment.

A single pawn can turn into a queen, proving once again that glow-ups are real.

Checkmate is that magical moment when your opponent suddenly becomes very interested in rematching “just for fun.”
